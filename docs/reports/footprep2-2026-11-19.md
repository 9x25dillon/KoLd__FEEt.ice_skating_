## What changed

### Part 1 — Crossed placement (`crossPlaceMode`, sim + pose lab)
- **`tools/ice-lab/sim/params.ts`**: added `crossPlaceMode: number`, set to **0 in every preset and setup**, validated in `validate()`.
- **`tools/ice-lab/sim/types.ts` & `sim/solver.ts`**:
  - Added optional `SkaterState.crossed?: boolean` (absent when `crossPlaceMode === 0`, so off-mode state and replay digests are byte-identical to `main`).
  - Added authored crossing clearances `CROSS_HALF_WIDTH = 0.06` m (12 cm lateral separation between blades) and `CROSS_HALF_ALONG = 0.10` m (20 cm fore-aft separation, outside blade ahead, inside blade pushing under behind; 23.32 cm contact-to-contact clearance, zero blade interpenetration).
  - During a crossover under-push (`s.crossover && s.crossSide !== 0 && s.strokeFoot === inside`), `s.crossed` is set true. `stanceOffset` and `stanceAlong` place the outside blade past the skating blade's line on the far side, share-weighted so `cop` in `sim/support.ts` follows the pendulum base. `copMax` uses `CROSS_HALF_WIDTH` when crossed, and `slipSolveFeet` uses the crossed lever arm.
  - Uncrosses cleanly on the next outside push, move, air, fall, or when shifting back onto the inside foot without a new under-push — avoiding any unloaded-contact jump at lift-off.
- **`tools/ice-lab/game/footprep.ts`**: added `FOOT_PREP.crossOver` (`out: -0.12`, `ahead: 0.20`, `lift: 0.12`), used only when `crossPlaceMode` is on (`s.crossed !== undefined`). Never invents a crossing when the mode is off.
- **`games/ice-run-godot/scripts/skater.gd`**: gated `contact_slide` on `blade_was_on_ice[i]` so a blade touching down from the air onto a crossed contact lands directly on its contact instead of sliding from its old unloaded position.
- **`games/ice-run-godot/tools/pose_clip.mjs` & `pose_lab.gd`**: added `--cross-place` to `pose_clip.mjs`; `pose_lab.gd` tracks `sim_crossed_frames` from the sim's contacts, keeps `crossed_frames == 0` when mode is off, and checks `crossed_frames == sim_crossed_frames` when mode is on.
- **Stills**: before/after boots-view stills (`crossPlaceMode` 0 vs 1 at ticks 150 and 186) saved in `docs/presentation/footprep/crossplace-1-outside-foot-crossing.jpg` and `crossplace-2-placed-during-push-under.jpg`.

### Part 2 — Glide recovery by situation (presentation only)
- **`tools/ice-lab/game/footprep.ts`**: added `"extended"` recovery (`FOOT_PREP.extended = { out: 0.06, ahead: -0.44, lift: 0.18 }`), chosen strictly from present state (`g.phase === "one"`, `s.strokeTime <= 0`, speed, carrier blade dwell, and carrier blade tilt/edge depth) with named authored thresholds:
  - `EXTEND_SPEED = [1.5, 3.0]` m/s
  - `EXTEND_FLAT_DWELL = [1.15, 1.65]` s
  - `EXTEND_EDGE_DWELL = [0.45, 0.90]` s
  - `EXTEND_EDGE_TILT = [0.08, 0.21]` rad
  - `EXTEND_ARC_MAX = 0.04` m (gentle shift along curved tracings, clamped below `extended.out` so it never crosses the skating line)
- Smoothly interpolates `beside` ↔ `extended` via `lerp` on `(out, ahead, lift)` with zero pops. Keeps `beside` for stroking rhythm and placement (`taking`). Zero sim changes; digests byte-identical.

## Recommendation: which setups to turn `crossPlaceMode` on in

From the numbers:
- **Simulation, Experimental, and Dig Gate**: **recommend turning on (`crossPlaceMode: 1`)** once the owner judges the feel in play. All three already run `weightTransferMode: 1` (0.45 s smooth hand-over), so the crossed contact enters and exits with zero step pop (8.8 cm max foot step, 2.2 cm off-line, 54/54 crossed-frame parity in pose lab, 23.32 cm blade clearance). Forward crossover speed gain (+0.262 m/s), curve held (71.71°), transfer timing (0.45 s), and falls (0) are unchanged, and the canonical 2Lo entrance still lands clean (1.933 rev, L 23.58 vs 24.17; note that pinned `backjump.test.ts` checks `|L - 24.17| < 0.1`, so turning it on in Experimental will require re-measuring that pinned L value).
- **Blade Explorer and Full Repertoire**: **keep off (`crossPlaceMode: 0`) for now**, or turn on only together with `weightTransferMode: 1`. Without weight transfer, contacts switch instantaneously (0.20 s two-foot block), so jumping directly into and out of a crossed stance relies on `contact_slide` smoothing rather than a physical hand-over.
