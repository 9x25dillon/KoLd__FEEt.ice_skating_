# The body sways over a planted blade (`comSwayMode`) — 20 November 2026

**Status: built, off by default and in every setup.** Replays stay at `/39`. With the mode off,
every replay digest is byte-identical. Tests: `tools/ice-lab/test/comsway.test.ts`.

## What it changes

With `weightTransferMode` on, a weight shift used to slide both blades sideways under a body that
kept its own path: up to the stance's width (24 cm), so the blades' tracing smeared on every shift.
With `comSwayMode: 1`:

1. **The body sways over the blades.** Each tick the legs move the load, the body moves the other
   way by the same amount, so the blades stay where they were planted.
2. **The load follows a minimum-jerk curve.** It runs over `comSwayTransferTime` (0.35 s,
   **authored**), re-planned smoothly whenever the asked load changes. The old critically damped
   response starts at its peak acceleration, which would have needed 10.8 g of sideways push for
   half the load and 21.6 g for all of it. The plan's own peak for a whole-load shift is 1.15 g.
3. **The push goes into the blades, capped at their bite.** The stance takes the opposite push.
   Each tick, the push is capped at what every carrying blade can still hold by the slip solve's
   own rule, so the sway never skids a blade by itself. What the cap holds back, the body doesn't
   get, and the blades slide by that much instead. The legs also push no harder than the plan's
   peak.
4. **No momentum is lost when the sway stops.** When a move takes the foot, or the skater leaves
   the ice or falls, the body's sway velocity goes back into the skater's velocity.

- **On an edge,** the blades stay planted and the body sways over them.
- **On flat blades,** the bite caps the sway at about half of the planned 24 cm, gently (0.13 g),
  and the blades still slide by the rest. The sim gives a flat blade very little sideways bite.
- **Falls:** none new. The only fall in the measured set is a 46° lean at 3 m/s, which falls with
  the mode off too.
- **What remains on a curve** (about 0.1–0.2 cm per tick) is the lean model, not the weight shift.
  A lean change slides the base under the body with the weight held on one foot as well.

### Dead ends on the way (don't retry)

- **A third-order filter for the transfer.** Settling in 0.35 s forces a fast start (about 4 g
  peak). Minimum jerk gets 1.15 g.
- **Charging the full push to grip, uncapped.** 93 tests failed. A shallow edge early in a curve
  (9° of lean) can't hold the push, so the blade skids, and the sim's skid doesn't recover: the
  stance kept sliding at 0.35 m/s, the carve was lost and the lean ran away.
  `takeoff-orientation.test.ts` stopped loading because its scripted takeoff fell; that was a fall,
  not a NaN.
- **Letting the held sway force reach the lean equation.** It counted the body's push a second
  time, as a shove at the pendulum's base. The two legs carry it, and the trunk doesn't tilt.

## With the mode on in Simulation, Experimental and Dig Gate: 67 tests move (not shipped)

- **Scripted jumps (about 25).** The canonical double loop leaves with L 21.17 (was 24.17) and
  comes up short (1.76 rev). Power's entrances fall, and its triple loop doesn't leave the ice. The
  "Turn in the air" lesson and the career "Jump accent" goal fall. These scripts are knife-edge
  search results. Varying only the transfer time gives L 23.8 at 0.15 s, a fall at 0.20 s, 16.6 at
  0.25 s and 21.2 at 0.35 s, because it moves when the load reaches the jumping foot (62 % at load
  start with the mode off, 89 % with it on). Turning the mode on needs a JPSE re-search, as weight
  transfer did.
- **Tutorial lessons:** all still pass, 0.02–0.34 s sooner, except Experimental's "Half a lap",
  0.49 s later.
- **Drifted numbers (about 20):** stroke and snowplow speeds, wear and rust counts, novice falls,
  pad-mapping traces, spin and dig wind-up, and the JPSE and conformance fixtures.
- **Two tests compare slip on with slip off.** The sway runs only with slip on, so these differ by
  design.

## Before turning it on: revisit these

1. **`crossPlaceMode` crosses fewer ticks.** The 0.35 s transfer reaches "outside foot carries more
   than half" later in the under-push, so the 140/140 crossing check fails. Decide the gate and the
   transfer time together.
2. **Katya Vell's boards gap.** In the warm-up pilot she ends 2.4 mm outside the boards, past the
   2 mm allowance kept on 20 November (boards snap off, choice a).
3. **Feel the 0.35 s transfer on the pad first,** and only then run the JPSE jump re-search. The
   jumps are sensitive to the transfer time, so pick it on purpose before searching against it.
