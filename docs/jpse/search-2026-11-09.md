# JPSE-007 first measured grid: 9 November 2026

`npm run jpse:search` from `tools/ice-lab`. The canonical loop entrance
(`jpse/baseline.ts`, Experimental setup, on a sheet) is varied over its own pad-script
knobs and walked in declared order with no randomness. Each point runs through the
unmodified solver and faces the slice's hard gates.

| knob | values |
| --- | --- |
| speed, m/s (backward) | 6, 6.5, 7, 7.5, 8 |
| load, s | 0.4, 0.5, 0.6 |
| checkAt, s into the air | 0.35, 0.375, 0.4, 0.425, 0.45 |
| depth, share of full stick | 0.8, 0.9, 1 |

## Cost and repeatability

- 225 candidates plus 10 repeats, 55,577 solver ticks. **62 ms per candidate** on this
  machine (14.6 s in total). Wall time is reported, never stored: it isn't
  deterministic.
- **10 of 10 repeats gave identical replay digests.**

## How the candidates failed (first failed gate)

| outcome | count |
| --- | --- |
| accepted | 2 |
| `takeoff:none`: the load lapsed | 175 |
| `landing:fell` | 30 |
| `takeoff-contact:SKID` | 15 |
| `landing:stood-`: down inside the run-out | 3 |

`takeoff:none` breaks down cleanly by knob. All 90 candidates at 6 or 6.5 m/s
and all 75 at 0.8 stick depth fail to take off. This setup's loop needs both the
speed and the deep edge before its load becomes a jump. That's measured here, and the
solver rule behind it hasn't been traced yet.

## Accepted candidates, and the Pareto front

| # | speed | load | check | depth | called | turned | takeoff q | landing q |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 104 | 7 | 0.4 | 0.45 | 1 | Lo | 1.960 | 0.817 | 0.519 |
| 113 | 7 | 0.5 | 0.40 | 1 | Lo | 1.971 | 0.619 | 0.602 |

\#113 is the baseline itself. Both points are on the Pareto front (rotation error,
takeoff quality, landing quality), so neither beats the other on all three. Choosing
between them needs weights, and that is the owner's decision. This pass doesn't make
it.

## What this says about the optimizer

The accepted region is tiny (2 in 225, close to the 2 in 10,080 that
`test/backjump.test.ts` measured), and its edges are hard gates, not slopes. A local
optimizer started off the region gets no gradient to follow. The next search should
therefore sample the knobs that matter (speed ≥ 7, depth 1, load and check) more
finely before any refinement step is chosen.
