Continuous controls

Explorer/Repertoire: left stick leans and pitches. Simulation: one stick per blade, hold the modifier for arms.
RT: load → release to jump. LT: brake (Simulation: LT/RT are the left/right knees; stops come from the feet and edges).
Simulation feet — D-pad nudges: ←/→ turn both feet, modifier + ←/→ toes in/out, ↑ straightens. Stick up/down: each stick's up/down turns its foot, heel/toe under the modifier. Modifier + left stick: x turns both feet, y toes in/out, the left blade keeps its edge. Keyboard: [ ] turn, - = toes, \ straightens.
LB / RB: select and retain left / right foot. Both: shared weight.
Right stick: arms and wind-up; forward during a spin selects camel. RT during a spin selects sit.
Keyboard: A/D lean, W/S pitch, Q/E foot, Shift load, X brake, C arms, comma wind-up.
