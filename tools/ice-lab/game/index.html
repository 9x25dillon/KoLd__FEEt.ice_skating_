EDGEWORK / OPEN PRACTICE

Feel the edge.

A fresh sheet of ice. Room to find your balance. Carve, turn backward, jump and spin — then put it all together.

Start with A / D to carve. Cruise handles the pushes while you learn. Follow the practice tips, or switch it off and skate your own way.

Every edge. Every move.

All three schemes use assisted balance, damping, recovery authority and blade control. Pick one mapping at a time. Jumps, turns, spins, twizzles and crossovers are enabled in every scheme. The close camera follows velocity, including backward travel, rather than body rotation.

Free Skate starts with Cruise on: automatic pushes below 5.5 m/s while gliding. Switch it off for manual strokes. Hold Space / A to repeat pushes. V or the camera button switches the view; mouse wheel zooms. Sound is optional.
The clock pauses while this guide is open. Moves need speed and a suitable edge; build speed first. The move display shows what you actually perform.
Pick a track above — Sound turns its playback on. Every crossover push lands in a beat window: on tempo is full strength, off it is weaker with an audible chop, never a failed push. A three-turn or a jump landed near the beat earns musical credit, shown as "Musical credit" — an early layer with more to come. The boards bounce a soft hit back onto the ice; a hard one is a fall.

Jump type comes from your takeoff foot, direction, edge and toe pick, rather than a jump-selection button. A three-turn reverses your body while you keep travelling; the camera follows that travel; your pushes then work backward.

Cantilever is a simplified animated low-back pose using the existing two-foot glide and knee compression. Dedicated cantilever balance physics and advanced spin variations are not implemented.
