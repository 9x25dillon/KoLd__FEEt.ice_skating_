    YOUR TIME ON THE ICE

    Find your signature.

    Preset looks. Your skating. Change your outfit anytime.

    Every edge. Every move.

    Choose a complete setup above, or use a legacy mapping override. Simulation removes jump coaching, wind-up assistance and automatic strokes. Blade Explorer keeps your edge choice with adjustable support. Jumps, turns, spins, twizzles and crossovers are enabled in every scheme. The close camera follows velocity, including backward travel, rather than body rotation.

    The three setups start with Cruise off. Optional Cruise adds: automatic pushes below 5.5 m/s while gliding. Switch it off for manual strokes. Hold Space / A to repeat pushes. V or the camera button switches the view; mouse wheel zooms. Sound is optional.
    The clock pauses while this guide is open. Moves need speed and a suitable edge; build speed first. The move display shows what you actually perform.
    Pick a track above — Sound turns its playback on. Every crossover push lands in a beat window: on tempo is full strength, off it is weaker with an audible chop, never a failed push. A three-turn or a jump landed near the beat earns musical credit, shown as "Musical credit" — an early layer with more to come. The boards bounce a soft hit back onto the ice; a hard one is a fall.

    Manual moves and blade control

    Simulation: left/right sticks control left/right blade lean; their average fore–aft input controls rocker pressure. Hold L3 (or your modifier) to use the right stick for arms, wind-up and camel; the right blade retains its last command. Keyboard: A/D for left blade, arrows for right; W/S for pressure, C for arms, comma for wind-up. Explorer: left stick leans and pitches; right stick controls arms.

    RT loads the knee; release to jump. LT brakes. LB/RB retain the chosen foot; both share weight. A pushes (Simulation requires each stroke). B turns: tap for a three-turn, transfer feet for a mohawk, hold for a loop, reverse lean for a rocker; combine transfer and reversal for a choctaw. D-pad down requests a bracket; hold and reverse lean for a counter. X twizzles, Y spins, R3 plants the toe or changes spin foot. Modifier + X/Y/A holds Ina Bauer/spiral/cantilever. Turns need speed and an edge.

    Full repertoire

    Left stick leans and pitches. RT loads and releases a jump; LT only brakes. Right stick opens the arms and winds up. While spinning, RT selects sit and right stick forward selects camel. LB / RB select and retain the skating foot; both share weight. Turns need an established edge. Each turn binding handles its hold, transfer or reversal gesture.
